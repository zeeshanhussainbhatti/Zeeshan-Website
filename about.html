Who I Am

Hi, I'm Zeeshan Hussain Bhatti

A professional digital marketing expert from Karachi, Pakistan — obsessed with turning clicks into customers.

My journey started more than 13 years ago, when most Karachi businesses still relied on newspaper ads and billboards. I saw early how Google and social media would change everything — and I've been helping brands ride that wave ever since.

Today I work with startups, local shops, clinics, real estate developers, e-commerce stores and corporate brands. My approach combines technical SEO, data-driven advertising and strong creative design, so every rupee you invest works harder.
