How to Scale an E-commerce Store in Pakistan Profitably

Zeeshan Hussain Bhatti · 8 Aug 2026 · 9 min read

How to Scale an E-commerce Store in Pakistan Profitably — featured image

Scaling an online store in Pakistan has unique challenges: cash on delivery, high return rates and fast creative fatigue. Here's the playbook I use.

1. Fix tracking first

Set up the Meta Pixel with Conversions API and GA4 e-commerce events. Without accurate data, every scaling decision is a guess.

2. Reduce COD returns

Confirm every order on WhatsApp before dispatch, offer a small discount for prepaid orders and set clear delivery timelines.

3. Test creatives weekly

Launch three to five new creatives every week. Winners are scaled gradually; losers are paused fast.

4. Build retention

WhatsApp broadcasts and email flows turn one-time buyers into repeat customers — the cheapest sales you'll ever make.
